# ROSA preview design system

Proposed redesign, awaiting Rodrigo's review. This is not a rebrand or a production approval.

## Direction

A product studio for the connected padel court. The original rotating court occupies an unframed, full-width stage beneath the principal product name. The story moves from court to real match proof, hardware choice, actual software and the club conversation. Large quiet fields and fine rules replace floating card stacks.

## Typography

Self-hosted Pretendard Variable remains the brand type. Display72/48, section48/34, subhead24/22, body18/16, secondary14. Use breakpoint steps, never viewport-scaled type. Letter spacing0. Text measure68ch, bold500-650 with700 reserved for principal headings. No mono costume or decorative uppercase kickers.

## Colors

Light: background#fafafa, foreground#161618, secondary#56565d, rule#dedee2. Dark: background#0b0b0d, foreground#f5f5f7, secondary#a4a4ad, rule#303035. Brand primary#cc0072; accessible foreground white. Green is availability/pre-sale only. A dark match-proof band provides contrast in either theme. Pink is an accent, not a wash across every surface.

## Layout

Maximum content width1320px with24/48px gutters, generous96/112px section separation,16/24px within groups. Product photography keeps its complete aspect ratio. Feature lists use shared rules and real hierarchy rather than framed cards. Real tool captures retain clear demo context and link to their working demo.

## Components

72px navigation, clear current-page treatment, theme/language controls and accessible product menu. Commands use restrained6px radius and48px target; icon-only tools have accessible names. Tabs use an underline, not floating pills. Contact keeps its native labels, consent, validation and existing submission behavior.

## Motion and accessibility

Keep the original rotating court as the authored motion; reduced-motion users get a static frame. Do not hide content behind entrance animations. Visible keyboard focus, native video controls, no page-wide overflow, logical focus order, persistent labels and sufficient contrast. Check desktop, tablet,320/390px, dark/light and all three navigation languages.
